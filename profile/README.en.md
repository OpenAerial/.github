# OpenAerial

[中文](https://github.com/OpenAerial/.github/blob/main/profile/README.md) | [English](https://github.com/OpenAerial/.github/blob/main/profile/README.en.md)

**Open infrastructure for reproducible and extensible aerial intelligence.**

OpenAerial aims to lower the barriers to building, developing, training, and deploying aerial intelligence systems. Through reference UAV platforms, we progressively share our mechanical designs, system integration, software, and workflows for simulation, data, training, and deployment. Our goal is to help developers, researchers, and universities worldwide pursue autonomous flight research and applications at an affordable cost, while contributing reusable results.

[Project and product materials](https://github.com/Accelerate11/OpenAerial) · [OpenAerial 150](https://github.com/Accelerate11/OpenAerial/blob/main/products/openaerial-150/README.en.md) · [Contribute](https://github.com/Accelerate11/OpenAerial/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.en.md)

## What we are building

- **Reference platforms starting with mechanical design:** original structures, system BOMs, assembly guides, and commercial hardware integration.
- **Workflows from development to deployment:** device interfaces, firmware configuration, onboard software, and autonomous flight capabilities.
- **Research and experimentation infrastructure:** simulation, data, training, evaluation, and hardware validation.
- **Materials maintained together:** clear version relationships, reproduction entry points, and Chinese and English documentation.

These are development goals. Product documentation defines what is available and what has been validated.

## Build with us

Contributions are welcome across mechanical design, hardware integration, software, simulation, data, validation, and documentation.

- Understand the organization and contribution process: [Contributing](https://github.com/Accelerate11/OpenAerial/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.en.md).
- Report issues or discuss improvements: [Project issues](https://github.com/Accelerate11/OpenAerial/issues).
- Review asset scope and origins: [Licensing and provenance](https://github.com/Accelerate11/OpenAerial/blob/main/docs/licensing.en.md).

Project-authored designs and integration materials will be shared progressively. Manufacturer materials for commercial modules are referenced within their actual published scope. Applicable licenses will be recorded with the respective assets.
